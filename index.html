About

Development and application of advanced computational, statistical, and machine learning methodologies to analyze large-scale multi-modal cancer datasets.

Research spans three interconnected areas: (1) experimental design, quality control, removal of unwanted variation, and integrative analyses of bulk and single-cell multi-omics data including RNA-seq, ATAC-seq, CITE-seq, and spatial transcriptomics; (2) Deep learning for digital pathology, integrating histopathology imaging with genomic and transcriptomic data to advance cancer diagnosis, subtype classification, and treatment response prediction; and (3) pan-cancer transcriptional coordination during cancer initiation, progression, spanning normal, primary, and metastatic disease, with a focus on uncovering shared and context-specific molecular programs that drive tumor evolution and metastasis.

Research

Three interconnected areas

Methods

Multimodal integration & method development

Statistical and computational frameworks for quality control, removal of unwanted variation and integrative analysis of high-dimensional multi-omics and digital pathology data.

Details
  • Removing unwanted variation — RUV-III with pseudo-replicates of pseudo-samples (PRPS) for large-scale RNA-seq (Nat Biotechnol 2023).
  • Scalable normalization — RUVprps for bulk RNA-seq and fastRUV-III for atlas-level single-cell and spatial data; RUV-III-NB for single-cell counts.
  • Platform-specific methods — normalization for NanoString nCounter (Nucleic Acids Res 2019) and tools to explore and remove unwanted variation in TCGA RNA-seq (tcgaCleaner).
  • Data types — bulk and single-cell RNA-seq, ATAC-seq, CITE-seq, TCR/BCR, spatial and digital spatial profiling, exome sequencing, methylation.
Translation

Cancer systems biology & translational oncology

Integrative analysis of normal, primary and metastatic cancers to resolve transcriptional programs underlying tumour evolution, response and resistance.

Details
  • Metastatic cancer — multimodal integration of pan-cancer metastases for biomarker discovery, including removal of host-tissue background signal (RUVprps_Met).
  • Prostate cancer — consortium analyses of mutational processes, epigenetic inactivation and evolutionary divergence (Pan-Prostate Cancer Group).
  • Tumour immunology — immune infiltration and patient outcome in colorectal cancer.
AI

Computational pathology

Interpretable deep learning that combines histopathology imaging with genomic and transcriptomic data for diagnosis, classification and stratification.

Details
  • Goal — combine histopathology images with genomic and transcriptomic data.
  • Approach — interpretable deep learning for diagnosis, classification and patient stratification.
  • Status — an emerging direction that builds on the multimodal integration work above.

R package · bulk RNA-seq

RUVprps

Normalization and integration of large and complex RNA-seq data.

Details
  • Method — RUV-III with pseudo-replicates of pseudo-samples (PRPS), which needs no prior knowledge of biological or unwanted variation.
  • Scale — designed for large, complex RNA-seq studies.
  • Developed at WEHI and DFCI, 2024–2026. Preprint under revision.
GitHubPreprint
R package · single-cell & spatial

fastRUV-III

Fast normalization and integration of atlas-level single-cell and spatial data.

Details
  • Use — normalization and integration of atlas-scale single-cell and spatial transcriptomic data.
  • Related — RUV-III-NB for single-cell counts (Nucleic Acids Res 2022).
  • Developed at WEHI and DFCI, 2024–2026. Joint single-cell multimodal and spatial normalization manuscript in preparation.
GitHub
R package · metastasis

RUVprps_Met

Removal of host-tissue background signals from metastatic bulk RNA-seq.

Details
  • Problem — signal from the surrounding organ can mask tumour biology in metastatic samples.
  • Approach — extends the RUVprps framework to remove host-tissue background.
  • Developed at DFCI, 2026.
